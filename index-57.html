🎤 Live voice detection

Listens to the preacher and automatically finds references such as "John 3:16", "Psalm 23" or "Romans 8 verse 28 to 30". It also recognises verses spoken without a reference, such as "the Lord is my shepherd, I shall not want", and shows the book, chapter and verse. When the preacher names a translation (for example "in the NIV", "the AMPC" or "in Kiswahili"), the screen switches to that version.
